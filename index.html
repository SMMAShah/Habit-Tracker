    Juz of the month

    The juz follows the date: the 29th is juz 29. On a 31st, read whatever you like. In February the last day picks up the juz the month is short of.

    Memorising, and reciting at Zuhr

    The morning block is one job split three ways. Revise before you add.

    • 30 minManzil — old memorisation on a rotation, so nothing goes cold.
    • 30 minSabqi — the last seven days of new work, from memory. Skipping this is why hifz leaks.
    • 50 minSabaq — the new portion, built line by line, then joined.
    • 10 minDalail ul Khayrat — one part. Mondays are parts 8 and 1, so start earlier.

    At Zuhr you recite sabaq and sabqi to your teacher, about 20 minutes, Monday to Friday. Corrections go into tomorrow's sabqi, not a notebook.

    Start at a quarter page a day. Only increase if the manzil stays clean for two weeks. On boxing mornings, halve the sabaq and keep the revision full length.
